03 — Questions

Good to
know.

The short, honest answers. Including the one about permission.

Whatever the source exposes. YouTube often publishes 2160p (4K) and sometimes 4320p (8K); Instagram usually tops out at 1080p for Reels. Kaptr lists only the real streams — it never upscales or fakes a higher resolution.

The interface works instantly in your browser. Real downloads come from a tiny local engine (yt-dlp + ffmpeg) started with node server.js. Until that's running, the app previews results in demo mode and tells you clearly when a file isn't real.

Instagram restricts a lot of content to signed-in viewers. Public posts usually resolve without cookies; private or age-gated posts need your own exported cookies. Kaptr supports a local cookies file — it is never uploaded anywhere.

No analytics, no accounts, no server-side history. Downloads and your local history list live in your browser and on your disk. The only outbound requests are the ones needed to fetch the video you asked for.
